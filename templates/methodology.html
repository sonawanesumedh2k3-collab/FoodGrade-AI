FoodGrade AI Scoring Methodology

Mathematical definitions of the 6-dimension health evaluation and FSSAI statutory claim verification.

Data Center
Research Prototype Note: The A–E health grade is an objective research score based on FSSAI dietary thresholds, not an official medical endorsement or commercial certification.
Two Separate Assessments
  • Claim Grade (A–E): Checks whether detected marketing claims (e.g. "High Protein", "Low Sugar") are legally supported by label nutrition data under FSSAI Advertising and Claims Regulations (2018).
  • Health Grade (A–E): Evaluates overall nutritional balance using the transparent 6-dimension benchmark.
A product can make a 100% accurate claim without being healthy (e.g., zero sugar in a high-saturated-fat snack). The grades intentionally remain unmerged.

Nutritional Dimension Weight Target Direction

FSSAI Statutory Claim Verification Criteria
Claim Type FSSAI Statutory Standard (2018)
Low Sugar≤ 5 g / 100 g for solids, or ≤ 2.5 g / 100 ml for liquids
High Protein≥ 20% of RDA per 100 g (solids), ≥ 10% per 100 ml (liquids), or ≥ 10% per 100 kcal
High Fibre≥ 6 g per 100 g, or ≥ 3 g per 100 kcal
Low Sodium≤ 0.12 g sodium per 100 g or 100 ml
Low Saturated Fat≤ 1.5 g per 100 g (solids) or ≤ 0.75 g per 100 ml (liquids), subject to total trans-fat ceiling

These statutory criteria are established by FSSAI; evaluating compliance does not supersede general medical nutritional advice.
