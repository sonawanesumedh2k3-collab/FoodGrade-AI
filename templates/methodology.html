FoodGrade AI V2.5 Methodology

This version freezes the research methodology before NLP, web crawling, OCR, recommendation and ML are added.

Research prototype: The A–E health grade is a FoodGrade research label, not an official FSSAI rating or medical recommendation.

Two separate grades

  • Claim Grade: checks whether detected product claims are supported by available label data and applicable FSSAI claim criteria.
  • Health Grade: evaluates the nutrition profile separately.

An accurate claim does not automatically mean the whole product is healthy.

Health model weights

Claim verification examples

ClaimResearch reference
Low sugar≤ 5 g/100 g for solids or ≤ 2.5 g/100 ml for liquids
High protein20% of RDA/100 g for solids; 10%/100 ml for liquids; or 10%/100 kcal
High fibre≥ 6 g/100 g or ≥ 3 g/100 kcal
Low sodium≤ 0.12 g sodium/100 g or 100 ml
Low saturated fat≤ 1.5 g/100 g solids or ≤ 0.75 g/100 ml liquids, subject to FSSAI's additional condition

These are claim-use criteria from FSSAI; they are not themselves a universal definition of a healthy food.

Dataset labeling

Every product should keep the original source, normalized nutrition values, detected claim, claim status, evidence/reason, claim grade, health grade and data-quality information.
