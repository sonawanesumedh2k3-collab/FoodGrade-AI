About FoodGrade AI

Intelligent Food Product Quality Assessment System — Problem statement, methodology, architecture, and technology stack.

Research Prototype
1. Problem Statement

Consumers often find it difficult to evaluate packaged food products because product labels contain multiple ingredients, nutritional values and marketing claims.

FoodGrade AI provides an intelligent system for analysing food-product information and generating separate health and claim assessments.

2. Objectives
  1. Collect food-product information from multiple sources.
  2. Extract ingredients, nutritional information and claims.
  3. Apply NLP and ranking techniques to product content.
  4. Generate separate Health Grade and Claim Grade.
  5. Recommend similar food products using content-based similarity.
  6. Demonstrate machine-learning based health-grade prediction.

6. End-to-End Workflow
  1. User enters or uploads a food product.
  2. Product information is retrieved or extracted.
  3. Ingredients, nutrition and claims are identified.
  4. NLP normalizes and ranks relevant product information.
  5. FoodGrade calculates Health and Claim grades.
  6. Similar products are identified using content similarity.
  7. The ML module demonstrates health-grade prediction.
  8. V7 validates the major system components.
System Validation: The current V7 validation run successfully passed all 6 implemented component checks.
Research Note: Some prototype grading and machine-learning components use demonstration data and rules. Larger validated datasets and further evaluation can be incorporated in future development.
